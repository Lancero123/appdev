# Favorite Anime Series: Hunter x Hunter

**Hunter x Hunter** is a celebrated adventure series created by *Yoshihiro Togashi*. The story follows young Gon Freecss as he embarks on a journey to become a licensed Hunter and find his missing father.

> "You should enjoy the little detours to the fullest. Because that's where you'll find the things more important than what you want." — Ging Freecss

## Why It Stands Out
* **Brilliant tactical combat** utilizing the complex Nen system
* **Masterful storytelling** that subverts shonen tropes effortlessly
* **High emotional stakes** and deeply developed characters

## Main Characters
1. **Gon Freecss** — Determined protagonist with an unwavering moral compass
2. **Killua Zoldyck** — Prodigy assassin transitioning into a loyal friend
3. **Kurapika** — Last survivor of the Kurta Clan seeking vengeance
